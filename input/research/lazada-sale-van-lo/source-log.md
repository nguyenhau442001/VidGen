# Lazada sale van lo — Source log

Research date: 2026-08-14

## Editorial finding

The proposed claim "Lazada sale sập sàn nhưng vẫn lỗ" cannot safely be stated as a
current standalone financial fact. Alibaba does not publish a separate Lazada net-profit
line. Lazada reported its first EBITDA-positive month in July 2024, while Alibaba reports
Alibaba International Digital Commerce Group (AIDC) as a segment containing Lazada,
AliExpress, Trendyol, Alibaba.com, and other businesses.

The video should therefore frame the question historically and explain the accounting
paradox: high sale-day GMV does not equal platform revenue or profit.

## Sources

1. Alibaba FY2026 Form 20-F (SEC)
   - https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000119312526231755/baba-20260331.htm
   - AIDC includes Lazada alongside AliExpress, Trendyol, Alibaba.com, and other businesses.
   - Alibaba's revenue model includes merchant services such as CPC, CPM, and CPS/transaction
     fees. Disclosed cost categories include logistics, platform operations, customer service,
     traffic acquisition, and payment processing.
   - AIDC reported an adjusted EBITA loss for fiscal year 2026, but this is a multi-business
     segment result and must not be presented as Lazada's standalone loss.

2. Alibaba June-quarter 2024 results (SEC)
   - https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465924090102/tm2421791d1_ex99-1.htm
   - Lazada significantly reduced operating loss through improved monetization and operating
     efficiency. AIDC still reported a segment adjusted EBITA loss, but that segment was not
     Lazada alone.

3. Alibaba FY2023 annual report (official)
   - https://static.alibabagroup.com/reports/fy2023/ar/ebook/en/118/index.html
   - Alibaba attributed Lazada's narrowing loss to improved monetization through more
     value-added services and enhanced operating efficiency.

4. Lazada first monthly EBITDA-positive report
   - https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3274370/alibabas-lazada-posts-first-monthly-profit-amid-intense-competition-southeast-asia
   - Lazada CEO James Dong said the company recorded positive EBITDA in July 2024 for the
     first time. This was a monthly EBITDA milestone, not proof of sustained net profit.

## Claims deliberately avoided

- No standalone Lazada loss amount.
- No current claim that Lazada is still loss-making.
- No actual Lazada take-rate, voucher contribution, or per-order subsidy number.
- No claim that Lazada alone caused AIDC's reported segment loss.

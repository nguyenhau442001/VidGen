Privacy Policy

How VidGen Publisher handles information when creators connect TikTok and prepare a post.

Effective August 10, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to VidGen Publisher, a creator-facing tool for reviewing and publishing original short-form videos to a creator’s connected TikTok account. It covers the public website, Publisher workspace, TikTok authorization, and VidGen render handoff.

2. Information we process

TikTok account information

With your authorization, we process your TikTok open ID, access and refresh tokens, granted scopes, creator nickname, username, temporary avatar URL, available privacy levels, interaction permissions, and maximum video duration. We do not ask for or store your TikTok password.

Publishing content

We process the video you select, its filename, duration and media type, plus the title, caption, hashtags, privacy selection, interaction settings, AI-generated-content selection, commercial-content disclosures, confirmation, publish identifier, and processing status.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may process ordinary security and access logs such as IP address, browser type, request time, and error details. A signed, HTTP-only session cookie associates your browser with server-side tokens and drafts.

3. How we use information

  • Connect the TikTok account you choose and maintain the authorization you granted.
  • Display the correct creator identity and current posting controls.
  • Validate video duration, privacy, interactions, and disclosure choices.
  • Transfer content to TikTok only after your explicit confirmation.
  • Retrieve processing status and diagnose errors.
  • Protect the service from misuse and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information and do not use TikTok data for targeted advertising.

4. Sharing

We send your video and selected post settings to TikTok when you press the confirmed publish button. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process data only to operate and secure the service. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect users and the service.

5. Storage and security

TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored only on the server and encrypted at rest. The TikTok client secret is held in server environment variables and is never sent to browser code. We use signed HTTP-only cookies, HTTPS in production, OAuth state validation, access controls, and limited-scope credentials. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

Account tokens are retained while your TikTok connection remains active and are deleted after disconnection, subject to short-lived backups. Uploaded working videos and drafts should be removed after publication or 30 days, whichever comes first. Security logs may be retained for up to 90 days. Legal obligations may require a longer period.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit or abandon a draft before publishing, disconnect TikTok in the Publisher, and revoke access in TikTok’s app-permissions settings. You may request access, correction, deletion, or information about our processing by contacting us. Rights vary by location.

8. International processing and children

Service providers may process information outside your country under appropriate safeguards. VidGen Publisher is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the product or law changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.

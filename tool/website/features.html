How it works

A publishing workflow with the creator in control.

VidGen Publisher connects finished original content to TikTok while keeping every consequential choice visible and editable.

STEP 01

Import

Select an MP4, MOV, or WebM from your device, or open a secure VidGen handoff containing a render, proposed caption, hashtags, and metadata.

STEP 02

Connect

Authorize TikTok through OAuth. Tokens stay encrypted on the server and the connected creator’s nickname is shown in the Publisher.

STEP 03

Refresh

The Publisher queries current creator information for the available privacy levels, interaction permissions, and maximum video duration.

STEP 04

Review

Watch the video, edit caption and hashtags, choose privacy, and opt into comments, Duet, or Stitch only when supported.

STEP 05

Disclose

Identify self-promotional or branded content and review the applicable TikTok music and branded-content declaration.

STEP 06

Confirm

Explicitly confirm the final post. Only then does VidGen Publisher initialize transfer and show TikTok’s processing status.

Designed around creator agency.

  • No unattended or blind auto-posting
  • No preselected privacy level
  • Interactions off by default
  • Unsupported controls disabled
  • Editable proposed copy
  • Fresh account constraints before publish
  • Clear commercial disclosure
  • Real status from TikTok in live mode

Privacy Policy

VidGen — Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VidGen ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

DataPurposeRetention
TikTok access_tokenTo post videos to your TikTok account on your behalfSession only — not stored permanently
TikTok open_idTo identify your TikTok account during the sessionSession only — not stored permanently
Video file & captionTransmitted to TikTok API at upload timeNot stored by us after upload

We do not collect your name, email, password, payment information, or any other personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data listed above is used solely for the purpose of posting video content to TikTok on your behalf. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core functionality of the App.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties, except as required to operate the TikTok Content Posting API (i.e., transmitting the video and token directly to TikTok's servers).

4. Data Security

We handle all tokens and data in memory only. No credentials or user data are written to disk or transmitted to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

5. Your Rights

  • Revoke access: You can revoke the App's permission to post on your behalf at any time via TikTok Settings → Security → Authorized Apps.
  • Data deletion: Since we do not store your data, there is nothing to delete on our end. Revoking access is sufficient.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by updating the date at the top of this page.
